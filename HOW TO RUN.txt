Restock bot (One Piece + Pokémon) - how to run it

Start it, or restart it after an update:   double-click restart_bot.bat
Make it start by itself at every login:    double-click install_autostart.bat (once; it also starts it now)
Stop it starting by itself:                remove_autostart.bat

Within a minute or two, Discord's #bot-status shows "Bot online (PC, v2.19.5)".
Pokémon channels: type /pokemon setup in Discord (once).
One Piece in-store channel: type /onepiece setup in Discord (once).

If something looks wrong, open bot_output.log in this folder and send Claude the last few lines.

New in v2.5: /watch add <link> <max> watches any product page; /ebay list|check|max|add|remove; /bought <card>.
New in v2.7: /value <card> shows what a single's worth at the shops now, with a price chart for the last week.
  For the chart picture, once:  py -3 -m pip install matplotlib   (without it /value shows a text sparkline).
  The week fills out over the first 7 days after updating (the bot used to keep only 2 days of single prices).

New in v2.8: TCGplayer prices (tcgplayer.py, via TCGCSV - TCGplayer's own data, refreshed daily, no key needed).
  The cloud copy downloads them every morning (~7:45am) and posts a daily report: biggest movers, singles
  cheaper at Elemental Arcade / Good Games than TCGplayer's market, booster box prices, and your want list.
  /tcgp <search> looks up any card or sealed product, e.g. /tcgp OP13-120 or /tcgp op13 booster box.
  /value now shows TCGplayer's market (in A$) under each version too.
New in v2.9: /pricecheck deck <deck> says what a whole deck costs to buy right now.
  Pick a saved deck or a want-list deck (your "deck" tags) from the list, or paste a decklist
  (1xOP12-001 4xOP12-016 ...). It shows the cheapest play copy in stock at Elemental Arcade / Good Games,
  what's not in stock, and the TCGplayer total. Collector versions (Wanted Poster, alt art...) don't count.
  /pricecheck save name:<name> list:<decklist> keeps a deck (or attach the .txt export) · /pricecheck list
  Saved decks live in decks.json; the updater never touches it.

  Its own channel (optional): double-click add_channels.bat, then add DISCORD_WEBHOOK_PRICES to GitHub
  as it says. Until then the report posts in the price-drops channel.
New in v2.10: One Piece in-store section (#onepiece-in-store), the One Piece twin of #pokemon-in-store.
  The evening before a release (6pm) it posts "tomorrow in stores"; at 7am on the day "out today" (pings the
  One Piece ping role), with what's out, which online shops have it right now and where to look on a shelf.
  /onepiece report  - spotted stock in a store? posts it there for everyone (store, what, price, photo).
  /onepiece instore - sightings from the last 24 hours.   /onepiece releases - the calendar + drop windows.
  Set it up once: /onepiece setup in Discord (admins), then add the DISCORD_WEBHOOK_INSTORE secret it shows
  to GitHub so the cloud copy can post when the PC is off. Until then the section is quiet (nothing else changes).
  Options in config.json, all optional:  "instore": {"evening": "18:00", "morning": "07:00", "ping": true,
  "online": true, "stores": ["EB Games / Zing", "Big W", "Kmart", "Target", "JB Hi-Fi", "your local game store"]}
New in v2.11: AUTO-BUY. The PC checks out restocks by itself (booster boxes and /watch links, Shopify shops,
  no pre-orders), within limits you set. It never types or stores your card: it uses its own Chrome profile that
  you sign in to Shop Pay with, once.
  Set it up once:  double-click setup_autobuy.bat. It installs what it needs and opens a Chrome window.
                   Sign in to Shop Pay (shop.app) with your address and card saved, sign in to any shops you
                   have accounts with, then close the window.
  Then in Discord: /autobuy try link:<a product page at a shop you buy from>  - a test run, never pays,
                   posts a screenshot of the checkout in #bot-status. Try each shop you care about.
                   /autobuy set mode:test  - real restocks go all the way to the Pay button, but no further
                   /autobuy set mode:live per_order:300 per_day:300  - really buys
                   /autobuy set mode:off   /autobuy status
  It stops and posts the checkout link for you whenever a shop wants a sign-in, captcha, card or address, or
  the total (with postage) is over your limits. If your bank asks to approve the payment, you get a phone push.
  Limits and options: "auto_buy" in config.json (see the top of auto_buy.py).
New in v2.11.1: the AUTO-BUY LIST. Auto-buy only buys what's on your list, each with its own max price and how
  many you want in total (it stops once it has them). It starts with OP-14 to OP-18 and EB-05 booster boxes at up
  to $260, 1 each.   /autobuy list  ·  /autobuy add code:OP-15 max:240 qty:2  ·  /autobuy add match:premium|prb-02
  max:300  ·  /autobuy remove.   Weekly and monthly budgets too (default $500 a week, $1,000 a month):
  /autobuy set mode:live per_week:500 per_month:1000
New in v2.11.2: AUTO-BUY CONTROLS. Two ways, same settings:
  Discord:  /autobuy panel  - buttons for Off / Test / Live, a dropdown of your items (change max / qty, pause,
            reset, remove), and Add item, Limits and Test a link forms. Works from your phone.
  The PC:   double-click open_autobuy.bat - a page in your browser with the same controls, spend bars for the
            day / week / month, the last attempts and the checkout screenshots. Only this PC can open it (with
            the key in autobuy_web_token.txt). For your phone on home Wi-Fi: "auto_buy": {"web_lan": true}.
New in v2.11.3: AUTO-BUY IS PRIVATE. Friends in the server can't see or use it, even if they're admins: only the
  server owner can (add others by Discord user id: "autobuy_user_ids": ["123..."] under "alerts"). Its results and
  checkout screenshots (which show your address) go to 🔒・auto-buy, a channel only you can see; the first
  /autobuy panel or /autobuy set makes it. Shared alerts no longer say a find is being auto-bought, and /status
  and /help don't mention auto-buy.
New in v2.11.4: MY COLLECTION. A second tab on the open_autobuy.bat page, just for you (nothing goes to Discord or
  GitHub). Add your tins, sealed product, cards and promos with what you paid. For each one it shows the price now,
  the price on the day you added it, the change over the last 7 days (with a little trend line), and your total
  cost, what it's all worth and how much you're up or down. How it prices things, from the bot's own records:
    a card - its number (e.g. OP05-119), plus words for the version ("alternate art"): cheapest copy in stock
             at the singles shops, else TCGplayer;
    sealed - words from shop titles ("ts-02 tin"): cheapest in stock across every shop the bot watches;
    either - or type your own estimate, for things shops don't sell.
  It checks every hour and keeps one price a day. Kept in collection.json and collection_photos/ (yours).
New in v2.19.5: SHOP LINKS IN CAPTCHA NOTICES. When shops ask the bot for human verification, each shop's name in
  the #bot-status notice (and in /captcha) is a link to that shop, so you can tap through and check it yourself.
New in v2.19.4: ONE PACE FOR ALL SHOPIFY SHOPS. Shopify counts requests from your internet address across every
  shop it hosts, so the bot now spaces its Shopify requests as one group: boxes and sealed first (8 a second),
  singles and price-drop lists only in the gaps (4 a second). If any Shopify shop says "slow down", every
  Shopify shop is slowed (down to a quarter) and singles wait 5 minutes; it speeds back up after 10 quiet minutes.
  Change with "shopify_pace": {"per_second": 8, "background_per_second": 4} in config.json, or false to turn off.
  bot_output.log shows a "Shopify shops:" line each check.
New in v2.19.3: SINGLES OUT OF AUTO-BUY. eBay deals (singles under TCGplayer market) now post in their own private
  🔒・deals channel, made when the bot starts; 🔒・auto-buy is only auto-buy again. Deals were always alerts only,
  never bought. Also: a single whose set name says "Booster Box" ("Franky OP18-021 [The Dominance of God Booster
  Box]") is no longer counted as a booster box, and auto-buy refuses anything with a card number.
New in v2.19.2: CHECK PRICE. Each item in My collection has a 🔄 Check price button. A card is looked up at
  Elemental Arcade and Good Games right then (cheapest near-mint copy in stock of that version); sealed items
  re-read the bot's latest shop check (every few minutes). The new price is kept for the rest of the day.
New in v2.19: THE SCORECARD - is the bot good enough to charge for? /scorecard show grades the bot against another
group's alerts (Fellowship), checks it was running every hour, and says ready / not ready against targets
(28 days of data, 30 drops compared, first or within 1 minute on 8 in 10, checking 99% of hours). Set it up once:
  1. /scorecard feed channel:#fellowship-feed   (make a private channel for it first)
  2. discord.com/developers > your bot > Bot > turn on "Message Content Intent", then run restart_bot.bat.
  3. In Fellowship's server press Follow on their alert channel and pick #fellowship-feed (or forward alerts there).
Every link posted there is graded 30 minutes later (so a slightly late bot alert counts as late, not missed) and the
bot replies under it. Right-click any message > Apps > "Grade against bot" does one by hand. Grade every alert, not
only the misses. A scorecard posts there every Monday morning. Settings: "scorecard" in config.json.
Also in v2.19: the auto-buy Chrome now runs with Chrome's sandbox ON (the "--no-sandbox" banner is gone).

New in v2.18: RESTOCK TRENDS PER SHOP. /restocks in Discord shows every shop busiest first: how many restocks in
  the last 30 days, up or down against the 30 before, a bar per week, how often it restocks and how long the stock
  lasts. /restocks shop:<name> shows one shop: weekly chart, busiest day and hour, a rough guess at the next
  restock and its last few restocks. days: changes the period (7 to 90), game: picks One Piece or Pokemon.
  Products that come in at one shop within an hour count as one restock. Read from events.jsonl (the bot's own
  log of in-stock / sold-out changes), so it needs nothing switched on, and the up/down comparison fills in once
  the log covers twice the period. Try it without Discord: py -3 restock_trend.py   or   py -3 restock_trend.py Pokebox
New in v2.16: CAPTCHA DETECTION IS ALWAYS ON (PC copy and cloud copy, no Docker or VPN needed).
  - When a shop answers with a "prove you're human" page, the bot stops checking that shop, tells you once in
    #bot-status, and waits. It never solves or gets round the check.
  - /captcha in Discord lists the paused shops. Open the shop in your own browser; if it lets you in,
    /captcha resume:<shop> (or resume:all) and the bot tries it on the next check.
  - A "slow down" challenge clears by itself: the bot waits 10 minutes, doubling up to 4 hours, and only tells
    you if it's still going after 6 hours. The cloud copy tries a paused shop once a day by itself.
  - To switch it off: "security": {"captcha": {"enabled": false}} in config.json.
New in v2.15: SECURITY (all off until you switch it on - nothing changes for the way you run the bot today).
  - run_docker.bat runs the bot in an isolated container behind a VPN container (Docker Desktop + a Mullvad account).
    The bot can then only write inside docker-data\, can't touch Windows, and has no internet except through the VPN.
  - open_security.bat opens the Security page: environment, VPN status, public IP, IP rotation, CAPTCHA pauses,
    monitoring, EMERGENCY STOP.
  - run_pc_side.bat keeps auto-buy working on the PC (your signed-in Chrome, your normal connection).
  - Without Docker you can still switch the checks on with "security": {"enabled": true} in config.json.
  Everything is explained in SECURITY.md.

New in v2.14: JB HI-FI. The bot now watches JB Hi-Fi's One Piece card products: new ones as JB lists them, and stock
and price for each (JB's own stock and its marketplace sellers). It reads JB every 10 minutes (every 2 in a drop window)
and takes about 9 hours after the first run to work through JB's back catalogue; older products it finds that way only
alert when they're buyable within your max. Off with "chain_shops": false in config.json, or pause "JB Hi-Fi" like any

New in v2.13: THE CLOUD COPY CHECKS EVERY 3 MINUTES. GitHub's timer was only starting a check every 4-7 hours.
The cloud copy is now one long job that checks every 3 minutes (every minute in a drop window, e.g. release eve and
release morning), singles every 15, and starts the next job itself every 50 minutes. A "Watchdog" restarts it if it
ever stops, and #bot-status gets a message if a job fails. Nothing to do on the PC. To stop the cloud copy: GitHub >
Actions > Restock check > "..." > Disable workflow (and the same for Watchdog). To change the pace: EVERY_MINUTES at
the top of .github/workflows/main.yml.

New in v2.12: DEAL FINDER (PC copy, private). Every 15 minutes it reads the newest One Piece singles on eBay Australia
  (Buy It Now, posts to Australia) and compares each one with TCGplayer's market for the same card and version, in A$.
  A listing 25%+ under market, delivered (item + postage + GST from overseas), with at least A$10 profit left after
  13% selling fees, goes to 🔒・auto-buy (only you can see it), and a phone push when the profit is A$25+.
  It never buys, bids or makes offers: you decide. It uses the same free eBay key as the eBay watch.
  It values every listing as the CHEAPEST version its title fits (no "alt art" in the title = the plain card), and skips
  Japanese, graded, lots/playsets, damaged, proxies, low-feedback sellers and anything under 20% of market (fakes).
  Always check the photos before buying.   Try it:  py -3 deal_finder.py   (prints today's deals, posts nothing)
  Settings, all optional, in config.json:  "deal_finder": {"min_pct": 25, "min_profit": 10, "min_market": 30,
  "sell_fee_pct": 13, "push_min_profit": 25, "every_minutes": 15, "searches_per_hour": 40}
  Posts in 🔒・deals ("discord_webhook_deals" under "alerts"), never in 🔒・auto-buy (v2.19.3).
