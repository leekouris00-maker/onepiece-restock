Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

In short: the app keeps only what you put into it, so your collection follows you between devices. There are no ads, no outside analytics or tracking companies, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. To run Rewards and see whether the app is useful, it keeps a simple count of a few things signed-in people do (explained below), in its own database only. Card scans are read on your phone and never uploaded. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who runs this

One Piece Market is a personal project run by an individual in Melbourne, Australia. It is not affiliated with Bandai, TCGplayer or eBay.

What is collected

Only if you create an account:

  • Your email address and password. Used to sign you in. The password is stored hashed by our login provider; we can't see it.
  • What you add to the app: collection items (names, quantities, prices you paid, your own values, groups, notes, and the date you sold something), sales you log, your own cards, the eBay searches you track, your watchlist and price alerts, and photos you choose to add.
  • Phone notifications, if you turn them on. Your browser gives the app a notification address for your device (a link to Apple's, Google's or Mozilla's notification service plus encryption keys) and we store it with the type of device (for example "iPhone"). It's used only to send your price alerts. Turning notifications off, or deleting your account, removes it.
  • Links you share. If you share a group of your collection, anyone with that link can see the cards in that group (names, card numbers, versions, quantities, condition and language) with today's prices. They can't see what you paid, your notes, your other groups or who you are. Stopping sharing in the app switches the link off.
  • Rewards, if you use them. Your daily check-ins (the date), the points you earn and spend and why (for example "Daily check-in" or "Swapped for A$5 store credit"), the milestones, weekly bounties and set-completion steps you've claimed, whether you turned on the evening streak reminder, and any swaps you ask for. Your bounty and rank are worked out from these. The name on your wanted poster stays on your device unless you share the poster yourself. This is needed so points follow you between devices and so the rules can be applied fairly (one check-in a day, one account per person).
  • A daily count of a few things you do in the app. For each day: whether you opened the app, and how many times you searched, added to your collection, scanned and added a card, set a price alert, imported or exported a CSV, or opened the trade calculator, deck price, set progress, sales report, watchlist, binders, top movers, Ban & Meta, promos, Rewards, your bounty poster or the points shop. Only those counts are kept, not what you searched for, which pages you scrolled, your location or anything about your device. It's used only to see whether the app (and Rewards) is working: how many people come back, and which features are used. It's not used for ads and isn't shared.
  • The app's owner can see totals and swaps. The owner sees overall numbers (sign-ups, how many people came back, points given out and owed, feature counts) and, for swaps, the email address of the person asking so the swap can be arranged. Nobody else can see another person's points, check-ins or counts.

Feedback you send (signed in or not): your message, the page you were on, your device type and screen size, a screenshot if you attach one (signed-in only), and your email address if you're signed in. It's only used to fix problems and improve the app.

Without an account you can browse prices, search and scan; nothing about you is stored on our side unless you send feedback.

What stays on your phone

  • The card scanner. The camera picture is read on your device to find the card number and name. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere. The app only uses the camera while the scanner is open.
  • Settings and saved copies, like your currency choice, today's price list, a copy of your collection (so it opens quickly and works without signal), recent searches, a card stack you haven't added yet, and calculator settings. Your sign-in is also remembered on the device until you sign out. Clearing the website's data in your browser removes all of these.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase stores your account and data, in its Sydney (Australia) region. Each account's rows and photos can only be read by that account (apart from the owner totals and swap details described above).
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app and the daily price files. Like any website host, it may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses).
  • TCGplayer's image server supplies card pictures, so it sees requests for them from your device.
  • CompsAPI receives the eBay search text for tracked cards and cards people own (for example "one piece OP13-120 Sabo") to look up recent sold prices. It receives no personal details.
  • GitHub Actions runs the morning jobs: it reads tracked cards, watchlists, price alerts and notification addresses from the database to look up eBay prices and send alerts. Nothing is kept there after a job finishes, apart from the public price files.
  • Apple, Google or Mozilla's notification services deliver phone notifications. The notification text (a card name and price) is encrypted on its way to your device.

How long it's kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. Items you remove are deleted straight away. Rewards points expire after 12 months without signing in. Deleting your account removes everything.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your login, collection, logged sales, own cards, tracked searches, watchlist and alerts, notification sign-ups, shared links, photos, and your binders, and your Rewards points, check-ins, bounties, swaps, reminder setting and usage counts. Feedback you sent is kept, but no longer linked to your account; ask if you'd like it removed too. If you can't sign in, ask using the contact options below and it will be done by hand.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and accounts shouldn't be created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: use Profile → Report a problem or idea in the app, or open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
