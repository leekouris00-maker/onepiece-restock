Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026

In short: the app keeps only what you put into it, so your collection follows you between devices. There are no ads, no analytics or tracking, and nothing is sold or shared for marketing. Card scans are read on your phone and never uploaded. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.

Who runs this

One Piece Market is a personal project run by an individual in Melbourne, Australia. It is not affiliated with Bandai, TCGplayer or eBay.

What is collected

Only if you create an account:

  • Your email address and password. Used to sign you in. The password is stored hashed by our login provider; we can't see it.
  • What you add to the app: collection items (names, quantities, prices you paid, your own values, groups and notes), sales you log, your own cards, the eBay searches you track, and photos you choose to add.

Without an account you can browse prices, search and scan; nothing about you is stored on our side.

What stays on your phone

  • The card scanner. The camera picture is read on your device to find the card number and name. Camera images are not saved or sent anywhere. The app only uses the camera while the scanner is open.
  • Settings and cached prices, like your currency choice and today's price list, so the app opens quickly. Your sign-in is also remembered on the device until you sign out.

Who handles the data

  • Supabase stores your account and data, in its Sydney (Australia) region. Each account's rows and photos can only be read by that account.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the app and the daily price files. Like any website host, it may keep standard server logs (such as IP addresses).
  • TCGplayer's image server supplies card pictures, so it sees requests for them from your device.
  • CompsAPI receives the eBay search text for tracked cards (for example "one piece OP13-120 Sabo") to look up recent sold prices. It receives no personal details.

How long it's kept

Your data is kept while you have an account. Items you remove are deleted straight away. Deleting your account removes everything.

Deleting your account

In the app, go to Profile → Delete my account. This immediately and permanently deletes your login, collection, logged sales, own cards, tracked searches and photos. If you can't sign in, ask using the contact link below and it will be done by hand.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and accounts shouldn't be created for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. Significant changes will be noted in the app.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on the project's GitHub page.
